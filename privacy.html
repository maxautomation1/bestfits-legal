Fashion Automation Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Fashion Automation ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

1. About This Application

Fashion Automation is a content automation tool that uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to automatically publish fashion outfit slider content to TikTok on behalf of authorized users.

2. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account to Fashion Automation, we access the following data via TikTok's official API:

  • user.info.basic: Your TikTok user ID, display name, and profile avatar
  • video.publish: Permission to directly post photo/video content to your TikTok profile
  • video.upload: Permission to upload content as drafts to your TikTok account

3. How We Use Your Data

Data collected through TikTok API scopes is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your identity and authorize posting on your behalf
  • Publish fashion content to your TikTok profile as instructed
  • Display your account information within the app interface

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the core functionality of the application.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your TikTok access tokens are stored securely and used solely to interact with TikTok's API on your behalf.

5. Data Retention

We retain your access credentials only as long as you use Fashion Automation. You can disconnect your TikTok account and request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Revoke TikTok authorization at any time via TikTok account settings
  • Withdraw consent at any time

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

8. Third-Party Services

Fashion Automation integrates with TikTok via their official developer API. Your use of TikTok is subject to TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or data requests, contact us at: customstud1os@gmx.de
